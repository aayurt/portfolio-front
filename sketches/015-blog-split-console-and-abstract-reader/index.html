Autonomy in the Clinic: The Research Frontier of Agentic AI

Aayurt Shrestha · March 25, 2026 · 12 min read
Abstract: Unlike reactive inference models that map inputs to static text, clinical agentic AI systems exhibit goal orientation, dynamic planning, and tool use. This paper surveys three critical frontiers for doctoral inquiry: long-horizon planning in stochastic care, mathematical bounding to prevent hallucinated actions, and multi-agent clinical consensus negotiation.
