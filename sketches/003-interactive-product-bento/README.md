## Variant: Interactive Product Bento (003-interactive-product-bento)

### Design stance
A product-first, modular Bento grid that elevates every platform to a first-class card with mini-simulators and responsive category filtering.

### Key choices
- **Layout:** Modern 3-column asymmetric Bento grid with high-impact 2-column span heroes for major architectures (Hermes and Nepse Pro).
- **Typography:** Contemporary neo-grotesque sans-serif with strong hierarchy and micro-metadata labels.
- **Color:** Strict grayscale palette with clean white containers on a subtle off-white background (#f5f5f5), sharp borders, and tactile card hover states.
- **Interaction:** Instant client-side filter pills (`All`, `Agents & AI`, `Offline & Systems`, `Web & Mobile`) with live category re-layout.

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Immediate scan-ability, product visual appeal, and modern portfolio aesthetic. Great for general tech company recruiters, founders, and product teams.
- **Weak at:** Less continuous narrative flow than an editorial format.

### Best for
- Senior Full-Stack, Product Engineer, and Tech Lead roles where versatility across AI, offline PWAs, and full-stack systems is valued.
