## Variant: Problem-to-Architecture Rails & Scholarly Research Feed (006-problem-solution-rails-and-research-feed)

### Design stance
A high-signal "Problem → Architectural Solution → Metric" horizontal rail system paired with a scholarly bibliography / chronological research feed for technical writing and papers. Built specifically for PhD admissions committees and enterprise technical directors.

### Choices made
- **Horizontal Problem-Solution Rails:** Rather than marketing cards, each solution presents an explicit engineering challenge ("Problem") paired directly with the technical countermeasure ("Pattern") and the resulting production metric (e.g. `< 15ms Rewrite Latency`, `91.4% Signal Accuracy`, `< 0.8s Scan Latency`).
- **Unified Scholarly Research Feed with Tag Filtering:** Formats blog posts as an interlinked research publication archive with category pills (`All`, `AI & Agents`, `DevOps & Infra`, `Systems & i18n`), reading times, and structured abstract summaries.
- **Strictly Grayscale:** Clean linear dividers, monospaced metadata labels, and minimal padding.

### Trade-offs
- Linear rails take more vertical scroll space than a 3-column card grid, but communicate engineering problem-solving clarity faster.
- Scholarly feed replaces flashy card graphics with dense, readable typography, favoring intellectual depth over consumer visual noise.
