## Variant: Scholarly Research Monograph & Systems Dispatch (013-blog-scholarly-monograph-and-systems-dispatch)

### Design stance
An authoritative research publication and systems engineering dispatch hub. Resolves the content tension by introducing a top-level two-track filter (`Doctoral Research & AI`, `Systems Architecture`, `Infrastructure & Cloud`). Elevated hero card for doctoral paper with abstract, formal methods diagram, and BibTeX export.

### Visual elements & hierarchy
- Category pills with count indicators
- Wide hero spotlight for clinical agentic AI paper with mathematical bounding diagram
- 2-column grid of engineering dispatches with framed architectural SVG visuals
- Estimated reading times and elimination of repetitive author avatars

### Trade-offs
- Richest representation for both PhD admissions committees and Senior/Staff Engineering leaders.
- Requires high-craft technical framing for all published articles.
