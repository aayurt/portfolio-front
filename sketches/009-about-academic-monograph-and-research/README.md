## Variant: Academic Monograph & Research Profile (009-about-academic-monograph-and-research)

### Design stance
A formal academic faculty and doctoral candidate profile. Strips commercial web flourishes in favor of scholarly typography (serif body, monospaced metadata, high-contrast black rules), interactive BibTeX citation drawers, and structured academic appointment listings.

### Choices made
- **Single-Column 820px Reading Measure:** Prioritizes uninterrupted academic reading with serif narrative paragraphs.
- **Interactive BibTeX Drawer:** Allows researchers to copy structured academic citations directly.
- **Strictly Grayscale:** Clean borders, monospaced metadata, zero color decoration.

### Trade-offs
- Best suited for academic applications, PhD committees, and research labs; less immediate "tech company recruiter" visual pop than the sticky rail.
