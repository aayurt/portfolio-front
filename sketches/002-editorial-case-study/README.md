## Variant: Editorial Case-Study & Research (002-editorial-case-study)

### Design stance
A scholarly, editorial, narrative-first structure that frames engineering achievements as research contributions and deep architectural case studies.

### Key choices
- **Layout:** Focused single-column reading column (840px max-width) with clear section breaks and progressive disclosure drawers.
- **Typography:** Serif body text (Charter/Georgia) paired with a clean, understated sans-serif system for meta labels, metrics, and technical diagrams.
- **Color:** Warm minimal paper background (#fbfbfb), deep charcoal text (#1a1a1a), and subtle architectural borders.
- **Interaction:** Collapsible "Examine Architecture" drawers that open technical data-flow proofs without derailing the narrative reading flow.

### Trade-offs
- **Strong at:** Academic rigor, deep context, clear problem-solution explanations, perfect for PhD applications and research labs.
- **Weak at:** Slower visual scanning for recruiters who only spend 15 seconds browsing.

### Best for
- PhD admissions committees, research engineers, and hiring managers evaluating system depth and clarity of thought.
