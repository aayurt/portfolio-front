## Variant: Capabilities Matrix & Editorial Ledger (004-solutions-matrix-and-editorial-blog)

### Design stance
A high-clarity systems engineering matrix for Solutions paired with an academic/publication Editorial Ledger for Blog posts. Puts concrete architecture deliverables first and frames writing as rigorous peer-level dispatches.

### Choices made
- **Solutions as 3-Up Matrix with Segmented Control:** Rather than generic service descriptions, each solution card features an interactive segmented switcher ("Architectural Deliverables" vs "Deployment Topology") allowing recruiters and technical leads to evaluate technical specs instantly.
- **Two-Tier Editorial Ledger for Blog:** Promotes high-gravity research (e.g. *Autonomy in the Clinic: Agentic AI*) as a flagship research feature card on the left, flanked by a dense, timestamped dispatch stack on the right.
- **Strictly Grayscale:** Uses neutral borders (`#e2e2e7`), mono tags, and high-contrast typography to emphasize layout architecture before theme injection.

### Trade-offs
- Prioritizes technical specification over client testimonials or marketing sales funnels.
- The 1.25 : 1 split ledger highlights the top 4 posts with high visual impact, but pushes older archive posts to a dedicated `/blog` archive page.
