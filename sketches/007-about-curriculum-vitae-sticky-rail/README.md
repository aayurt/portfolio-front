## Variant: Sticky Rail Curriculum Vitae & Categorized Competencies (007-about-curriculum-vitae-sticky-rail)

### Design stance
An engineering and academic CV profile that solves sidebar "dead space" via a sticky command rail (`Download CV`, `Get in Touch`, timezone, languages, and page navigation). Categorizes flat skill clouds into 4 clear technical pillars (`AI & Agents`, `Backend & Storage`, `Frontend & Mobile`, `DevOps & Systems`) and formats work experience into an impact timeline with quantifiable bullet points.

### Choices made
- **Sticky Left Rail:** Keeps identity, contact CTAs, availability status (`Open to Staff & PhD Roles`), and table of contents visible as visitors scroll.
- **Categorized Competency Quad:** Replaces 16 raw tags with 4 structured architectural domains.
- **Work Experience Impact Timeline:** Formats experience with clear bulleted accomplishments, metrics, and technical context.
- **Academic & Research Foundation:** Links education directly to doctoral research inquiries (*Autonomy in the Clinic: Agentic AI*).

### Trade-offs
- The 280px left rail requires a standard two-column layout on desktop, collapsing to a single column on mobile screens.
